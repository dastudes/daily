Daily Brief

Both NL East and AL West leaders lost ground Saturday, as Washington and Miami each pulled a game closer to Atlanta, while Texas closed to within a half-game of Houston in the AL West. The day's most dramatic finish came in Arizona, where the Dodgers edged the Diamondbacks 2-1 in a game with a 5.39 WPA swing to maintain their 8.5-game division lead.

  • The Dodgers beat the Diamondbacks 2-1 in the day's most dramatic game, dropping Arizona further back in the NL West. (DODGERS)
  • The Blue Jays outlasted the Phillies 7-5 in Philadelphia in another high-swing contest. (JAYS)
  • Sandy Alcantara [7 IP, 0 ER, 8 K | 3.53 ERA, PAR 42] dominated as the Marlins shut out the Angels 7-0, gaining ground on Atlanta in the NL East. (MIA)
  • The Nationals routed the Reds 8-2 behind a strong offensive showing, also gaining on the Braves. (BRAVES) (REDS)
  • The Rangers beat the Orioles 5-1 to pull within a half-game of Houston, who fell 3-2 to the Padres on Michael King [6 IP, 1 ER, 6 K | 3.37 ERA, PAR 34]'s strong start. (SD)
  • Jac Caglianone [4-4, 2 HR | RC 60] went 4-for-4 with 2 HR as the Royals downed the Cubs 6-3; Jake McCarthy [4-5, 2 HR | RC 64] also went 4-for-5 with 2 HR in Colorado's 8-6 win at St. Louis. (KC) (COL)
  • Gerrit Cole [7 IP, 2 ER, 9 K | 3.37 ERA, PAR 23] struck out 9 over 7 innings as the Yankees edged the Braves 5-4, with George Lombard Jr. [2-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 6] homering. (NYY)
  • Bubba Chandler [6 IP, 0 ER, 5 K | 4.26 ERA, PAR 23] tossed 6 shutout innings as the Pirates blanked the Mets 9-0, gaining ground in the NL wild (PIT)
