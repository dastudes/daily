Daily Brief

The Milwaukee Brewers pulled off the day's most dramatic escape, rallying from a 2% win probability to beat the Cubs 4-3 and push their NL Central lead to nine games. In the AL Central, both the White Sox's pursuers gained ground as Cleveland and Minnesota won, tightening the division race.

  • Jacob Misiorowski [6.1 IP, 1 ER, 9 K | 1.96 ERA, PAR 70] and the Brewers stunned the Cubs 4-3 after falling to a 2% win probability. (MIL)
  • The Diamondbacks-Royals game produced the day's largest WPA swing (5.06) in Arizona's 5-3 win. (DIAMONDBACKS)
  • Cam Schlittler [7 IP, 1 ER, 10 K | 2.02 ERA, PAR 73] dominated with 10 strikeouts as the Yankees beat the Rockies 5-3. (NYY)
  • Reid Detmers [6 IP, 0 ER, 9 K | 3.32 ERA, PAR 53] tossed six shutout innings and Zach Neto [4-5, 1 HR | RC 78] went 4-for-5 with a homer in the Angels' 6-1 win at Fenway. (LAA)
  • The Guardians routed the Orioles 9-5 and the Twins edged the Tigers 3-2, both gaining a game on the AL Central-leading White Sox, who lost to Pittsburgh 9-3. (GUARDIANS) (TWINS) (SOX)
  • The Mets beat the Marlins 7-5 to gain a game in the NL East, while the Rays cruised past the Braves 7-1 behind Junior Caminero [2-3, 2 BB, 1 HR | RC 109]'s homer. (TB)
  • The Astros outlasted the Phillies 6-5 as Christian Walker [2-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 67] and Daulton Varsho [3-5, 1 HR | RC 48] both homered. (HOU)
  • The Rangers pounded the Mariners 10-5, the Dodgers edged the Reds 3-2, the Giants and Padres won one-run affairs, and the Blue Jays beat the Athletics 4-2. (PADRES) (RANGERS) (JAYS) (GIANTS) (REDS)
