Daily Brief

Day 1 of the Division Series produced a mixed bag, with all four Game 1 winners now holding 1-0 series leads. The Brewers authored the day's most dramatic turn, climbing from a 27% win probability to beat the Padres 3-2 in the day's biggest WPA swing.

  • Brewers 3, Padres 2: Milwaukee rallied from a low of 27% win probability to take Game 1; William Contreras [1-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 80] homered. (MIL)
  • Rays 1, Yankees 0: Drew Rasmussen [8 IP, 0 ER, 10 K | 2.67 ERA, PAR 62] dominated with the day's top pitching line, outdueling Gerrit Cole [5 IP, 1 ER, 5 K | 3.61 ERA, PAR 31] in a tight Game 1. (TB)
  • Dodgers 5, Braves 3: Tarik Skubal [6 IP, 1 ER, 7 K | 2.73 ERA, PAR 59] turned in a strong start, while Teoscar Hernández [2-4, 1 HR | RC 63] and Kyle Tucker [2-4, 1 HR | RC 69] each homered; Ozzie Albies [1-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 71] went deep for Atlanta. (LAD)
  • White Sox 3, Guardians 0: Grant Taylor [4 IP, 0 ER, 6 K | 2.25 ERA, PAR 33] was sharp in relief/short start and Munetaka Murakami [2-4, 1 HR | RC 75] provided the power with a home run. (CWS)
  • All four Division Series stand 1-0 after Game 1; no team faces elimination in Game 2.
