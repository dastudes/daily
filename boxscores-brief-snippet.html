Daily Brief

The NL Central race tightened as the Brewers lost to the Reds while the Cardinals, Pirates, and Reds all picked up ground on Milwaukee. In the AL East, the Red Sox pulled within 6.5 games of the Rays, while the Braves staged the day's biggest comeback, rallying from a 5% win probability to beat the Phillies.

  • Ronald Acuña Jr. [2-3, 1 HR | RC 53] and the Braves stunned the Phillies 5-4 after falling to a 5% win probability, with Aaron Nola [7 IP, 1 ER, 5 K | 4.76 ERA, PAR 22] turning in a strong start in the loss. (ATL)
  • The Reds routed the Brewers 12-8 behind Elly De La Cruz [2-4, 2 BB, 1 HR | RC 87], cutting into Milwaukee's NL Central lead. (CIN)
  • Bryan Woo [8 IP, 0 ER, 9 K | 4.04 ERA, PAR 40] dominated in a 2-0 Mariners shutout of the Athletics, moving Seattle within 6.0 of the Astros in the AL West. (SEA)
  • Payton Tolle [6 IP, 1 ER, 12 K | 3.15 ERA, PAR 44] struck out 12 as the Red Sox beat the Orioles 3-1 to gain a game in the AL East. (BOS)
  • Jake Burger [2-4, 1 BB, 2 HR | RC 65] homered twice in the Rangers' 8-6 win over the Rays, pulling Texas to 2.0 back in the AL West. (TEX)
  • Walbert Ureña [8 IP, 1 ER, 4 K | 2.75 ERA, PAR 40] threw eight strong innings but the Angels lost 1-0 to the Pirates, who gained ground in the NL Central. (LAA)
  • Randal Grichuk [2-2, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 36] homered as the White Sox hammered the Twins 10-1. (CWS)
  • The Dodgers held off the Nationals 7-5 in the day's most dramatic game by WPA swing. (NATIONALS)
