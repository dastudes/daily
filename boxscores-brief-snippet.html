Daily Brief

The Milwaukee Brewers moved to the brink of a World Series sweep, routing the Tampa Bay Rays 10-1 in Game 3 to take a commanding 3-0 series lead. Tampa Bay faces elimination in Game 4.

  • Milwaukee Brewers 10, Tampa Bay Rays 1: The Brewers broke the game open with an eight-run fifth inning at Tropicana Field. (MIL)
  • Robert Gasser [6 IP, 0 ER, 3 K | 3.88 ERA, PAR 24] turned in the start of the night, blanking the Rays over six innings. (MIL)
  • Jackson Chourio [4-5 | RC 89] led the Milwaukee attack with a four-hit night. (MIL)
  • William Contreras [3-5 | RC 80] and Brice Turang [3-5 | RC 96] each added three hits in the rout. (MIL)
  • Christian Yelich [2-3, 1 BB | RC 53] chipped in a multi-hit game. (MIL)
