Daily Brief

The Mets battered the Braves 14-3 to cut Atlanta's NL East lead, with the Marlins also gaining a game on the leaders in a wild 8-7 win over Philadelphia. Meanwhile, the Cubs' 7-3 win at St. Louis pulled Chicago closer in the NL Central, while the Pirates' walk-off-caliber 3-2 win over Arizona further muddied the NL wild card picture.

  • Francisco Lindor [3-5, 1 BB, 2 HR | RC 26] powered the Mets' 14-3 rout of Atlanta, with Marcus Semien [3-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 31] also going deep as New York gained a game in the NL East. (NYM)
  • Miami edged Philadelphia 8-7 in the day's second-largest WPA swing, gaining another game on the Braves. (BRAVES)
  • Pittsburgh beat Arizona 3-2 in the day's most dramatic game by WPA, with Merrill Kelly [5.2 IP, 0 ER, 6 K | 4.87 ERA, PAR 9] turning in a strong no-run outing in the loss. (AZ)
  • Alex Bregman [4-5, 1 HR | RC 50] and Nico Hoerner [4-5, 1 HR | RC 46] each went 4-for-5 with a homer as the Cubs beat St. Louis 7-3 to gain a game in the NL Central. (CHC)
  • Cristian Javier [5 IP, 0 ER, 7 K | 7.25 ERA, PAR -1] led all pitchers in PAR with 5 scoreless innings as Houston beat the Angels 6-4. (HOU)
  • Kumar Rocker [6.2 IP, 2 ER, 7 K | 4.05 ERA, PAR 22] and Joc Pederson [2-4, 2 HR | RC 49] (2 HR) fueled Texas past Seattle 7-3, dropping the Mariners further back in the AL West. (TEX)
  • Baltimore, Boston, Toronto, and New York all won, each gaining half a game on AL East-leading Tampa Bay.
  • Cleveland at Cincinnati was postponed.
