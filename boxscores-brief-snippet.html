Daily Brief

The AL East and AL Central leaders both lost ground on a bad day for division front-runners, as the Rays fell to the Blue Jays and the White Sox were shut out by the Braves while Cleveland, Kansas City, Detroit, and Minnesota all gained. The Cardinals authored the day's most dramatic finish, rallying from an 11% win probability to beat the Reds 10-9.

  • Zach Neto [5-6, 1 HR | RC 65] powered the Angels to an 18-3 rout of the Astros, cutting into Houston's AL West lead. (LAA)
  • Jacob deGrom [6 IP, 0 ER, 10 K | 3.78 ERA, PAR 36] dominated as the Rangers shut out the Nationals 2-0, also gaining a game on Houston. (TEX)
  • The Cardinals stunned the Reds 10-9 in the day's biggest comeback. (CARDINALS)
  • Shane Bieber [7 IP, 1 ER, 7 K | 4.57 ERA, PAR 7] led the Blue Jays past the Rays 5-1, trimming Tampa Bay's AL East cushion. (TOR)
  • Gerrit Cole [6 IP, 1 ER, 8 K | 3.12 ERA, PAR 29] and the Yankees beat the Orioles 6-1 to gain a game in the AL East. (NYY)
  • The Guardians topped the Giants 5-2 and the Royals beat the Athletics 6-2, both gaining on the White Sox. (GIANTS) (ATHLETICS) (SOX)
  • Grant Holmes [6 IP, 0 ER, 3 K | 3.65 ERA, PAR 24] and the Braves shut out the White Sox 2-0. (ATL)
  • Garrett Mitchell [3-4, 1 HR | RC 55] homered as the Brewers held off the Mariners 7-4. (MIL)
