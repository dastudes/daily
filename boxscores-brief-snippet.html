Daily Brief

Three Division Series ended on October 7, with the Rays sweeping the Yankees, the Dodgers closing out the Braves, and the Brewers finishing off the Padres. The lone series still alive is in the AL Central, where the White Sox lead the Guardians 2-1 and Cleveland now faces elimination in Game 4.

  • Victor Mesa Jr. [1-4, 1 HR | RC 38]'s top-6th two-run homer put Tampa Bay ahead for good in a 4-3 win over the Yankees, completing a 3-0 sweep. (TB)
  • Andy Pages [2-3 | RC 82]'s top-7th two-run single broke open a 4-1 Dodgers win in Atlanta, clinching the NLDS 3-1. (LAD)
  • Fernando Tatis Jr. [1-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 105]'s bottom-3rd solo homer briefly tied it, but Milwaukee pulled away for a 3-1 win and a 3-1 series clincher over San Diego. (SD)
  • Jo Adell [2-4, 1 BB | RC 72]'s top-3rd three-run triple fueled Cleveland's 9-3 rout of the White Sox, the day's biggest comeback after the Guardians trailed 1-0; Chicago still leads the series 2-1. (CLE)
  • Tyler Mahle [6.1 IP, 1 ER, 4 K | 3.99 ERA, PAR 33] was sharp in a losing effort for Atlanta. (ATL)
  • Foster Griffin [2.2 IP, 1 ER, 7 K | 3.61 ERA, PAR 42] struck out seven in relief for Cleveland. (CLE)
