Daily Brief

The Atlanta Braves clinched the NL Wild Card Series with a 6-2 win over the Philadelphia Phillies in Game 3, eliminating Philadelphia and advancing to the NLDS. Atlanta rallied from behind in the decisive game, which also produced the day's largest WPA swing.

  • Braves 6, Phillies 2: Atlanta took the best-of-3 series 2-1, erasing an earlier deficit to clinch. (PHILLIES)
  • Matt Olson [1-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 101], Michael Harris II [1-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 93], and Ozzie Albies [1-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 71] each homered to power the Atlanta offense. (ATL)
  • Ray Kerr [3.1 IP, 0 ER, 2 K | 1.49 ERA, PAR 7] delivered the day's top pitching line out of the Atlanta bullpen, with Chris Sale [1.1 IP, 0 ER, 3 K | 2.16 ERA, PAR 68] adding scoreless relief work. (ATL)
  • Zack Wheeler [4 IP, 2 ER, 3 K | 3.00 ERA, PAR 51] was Philadelphia's best arm in the loss, and Kyle Schwarber [2-3, 1 BB | RC 103] led the Phillies at the plate. (PHI)
