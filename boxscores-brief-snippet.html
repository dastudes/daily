Daily Brief

The Tampa Bay Rays moved within one win of the pennant, taking Game 5 of the ALCS 3-1 at Progressive Field to push Cleveland to the brink of elimination. The Guardians now face elimination in Game 6.

  • Chase DeLauter [1-3, 1 BB | RC 82] drew a bottom-9th walk to bring the tying run to the plate, but the rally stalled as Tampa Bay closed out the 3-1 win and a 3-2 series lead. (CLE)
  • Drew Rasmussen [7 IP, 1 ER, 6 K | 2.67 ERA, PAR 62] was dominant for the Rays, carrying the game with seven strong innings of one-run ball. (TB)
  • Liam Hicks [2-3, 1 BB | RC 76] paced the Tampa Bay offense at the plate. (TB)
  • Yandy Díaz [3-5 | RC 98] added a three-hit day in the Rays' win. (TB)
  • Chandler Simpson [2-3 | RC 68] chipped in a multi-hit effort at the top of the order. (TB)
  • Steven Kwan [2-4 | RC 72] led the Cleveland offense in the losing effort. (CLE)
