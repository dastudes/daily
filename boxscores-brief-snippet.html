Daily Brief

Both NL West contenders gained a full game on the Dodgers, who lost at Arizona while San Diego handled Houston — tightening a division Los Angeles now leads by 7.5 over the Diamondbacks and 8.5 over the Padres. Philadelphia's one-run win over Toronto produced the day's biggest WPA swing at 5.64.

  • Kyle Schwarber [2-3, 3 BB, 2 HR | RC 82] and the Phillies edged the Blue Jays 7-6 in the day's most dramatic game, with Jesús Luzardo [7 IP, 2 ER, 12 K | 3.33 ERA, PAR 46] dominant on the mound. (PHI)
  • Arizona beat the Dodgers 4-2 and San Diego topped Houston 7-2, with both leaders — Los Angeles and Houston — losing ground. (DODGERS)
  • Pete Alonso [3-3, 2 BB, 2 HR | RC 72] powered Baltimore to a 10-5 win at Texas, gaining a game in the AL wild card race. (BAL)
  • Atlanta beat the Yankees 2-1 behind Cam Schlittler [7 IP, 1 ER, 11 K | 2.22 ERA, PAR 58]'s strong but losing effort; New York fell further back in the AL East. (NYY)
  • The Athletics stunned Boston 4-3 at Fenway, costing the Red Sox a game in the AL East. (ATHLETICS) (SOX)
  • The Mets routed Pittsburgh 11-1 as Sean Manaea [7 IP, 1 ER, 11 K | 4.15 ERA, PAR 21] dealt and Bo Bichette [5-5, 1 BB | RC 55] collected five hits. (NYM)
  • Milwaukee outlasted Minnesota 4-3, with Jake Bauers [2-3, 2 BB, 1 HR | RC 67] homering for the NL Central leaders. (MIL)
  • Detroit beat San Francisco 3-1 behind Logan Webb [8 IP, 1 ER, 2 K | 3.59 ERA, PAR 38]'s tough-luck start and Troy Melton [6 IP, 0 ER, 5 K | 1.46 ERA, PAR 31]'s scoreless outing, gaining a game in the AL wild card race. (SF)
