Daily Brief

The San Diego Padres' 6-5 win over the San Francisco Giants produced the day's biggest WPA swing at 4.50 and tightened the NL West, as San Diego, Arizona, and Colorado all gained a game on the Dodgers, who fell to Boston. In the AL Central, Detroit picked up ground on the White Sox with a win over the Athletics.

  • Drew Rasmussen [6 IP, 0 ER, 10 K | 2.92 ERA, PAR 39] led Tampa Bay past the White Sox 1-0, outdueling Chicago in a tight pitching battle. (TB)
  • Cristopher Sánchez [6 IP, 0 ER, 11 K | 2.62 ERA, PAR 54] and the Phillies shut out the Orioles 5-0 at Camden Yards. (PHI)
  • Hunter Goodman [4-5, 2 HR | RC 70] powered Colorado to a 12-6 win over Kansas City, with the Rockies gaining a game in the NL West. (COL)
  • Arizona outslugged Cleveland 12-8 behind Geraldo Perdomo [4-6, 1 HR | RC 50], gaining ground in the NL West. (AZ)
  • San Diego edged San Francisco 6-5 in the day's most dramatic finish, gaining a game on the Dodgers. (DODGERS)
  • Ceddanne Rafaela [2-4, 2 HR | RC 63] homered twice as Boston beat the Dodgers 3-2 at Dodger Stadium. (BOS)
  • Kevin McGonigle [3-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 70] and Framber Valdez [7 IP, 2 ER, 6 K | 4.42 ERA, PAR 23] led Detroit past the Athletics 8-6, gaining a game on the White Sox in the AL Central. (DET)
  • Martín Pérez [7 IP, 0 ER, 6 K | 3.24 ERA, PAR 24] tossed seven shutout innings as Atlanta rolled past Washington 8-3. (ATL)
