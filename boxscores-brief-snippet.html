Daily Brief

The Milwaukee Brewers rallied from a 4-run deficit to beat the Tampa Bay Rays 5-4 in Game 2 of the World Series, taking a 2-0 series lead. Milwaukee's win probability fell as low as 18% before the comeback.

  • Jonny DeLuca [2-4 | RC 45]'s leadoff double in the top of the 9th put the tying run in scoring position for Tampa Bay, but the Rays couldn't bring him home as Milwaukee held on 5-4. (TB)
  • Aaron Ashby [1.2 IP, 0 ER, 3 K | 3.13 ERA, PAR 28] turned in the day's top relief outing for Milwaukee. (MIL)
  • Sal Frelick [2-4, 1 HR | RC 32] homered as part of a multi-hit day for the Brewers. (MIL)
  • Liam Hicks [1-2, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 76] homered for Tampa Bay in a losing effort. (TB)
  • Jonny DeLuca added a two-hit game for the Rays. (TB)
