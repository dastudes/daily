Daily Brief

The Rays evened the ALCS at 1-1 with a 3-1 win over the Guardians at Tropicana Field, powered by a decisive fourth-inning rally. The best-of-7 series shifts with no team facing elimination in Game 3.

  • Liam Hicks [2-3 | RC 76] delivered the decisive blow in the bottom of the 4th, lining a two-run single to center that scored Chandler Simpson [1-3 | RC 68] and Ben Williamson [0-1, 1 BB] to put Tampa Bay up 3-0 in an eventual 3-1 win over Cleveland. (TB)
  • Freddy Peralta [6 IP, 0 ER, 6 K | 4.42 ERA, PAR 31] was dominant for the Rays, tossing six shutout innings with six strikeouts. (TB)
  • Parker Messick [5 IP, 0 ER, 3 K | 2.56 ERA, PAR 66] matched zeros for five innings in the loss, striking out three. (CLE)
  • Jonny DeLuca [2-4 | RC 45] and Hicks each had multi-hit games for Tampa Bay. (TB)
  • Chase DeLauter [1-2, 2 BB | RC 82] and Steven Kwan [2-5 | RC 72] led the Guardians at the plate. (CLE)
