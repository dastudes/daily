Daily Brief

The New York Mets outlasted the Miami Marlins 15-14 in the day's wildest game, but the bigger pennant-race news came in the NL East, where both the Mets and Phillies gained a game on the Braves after Tampa Bay knocked off Atlanta. In the AL West, the Astros lost ground to all three chasers as the Angels, Athletics, and Mariners each won.

  • Jackson Merrill [4-5, 2 HR | RC 70] powered the Padres past the Nationals 9-2. (SD)
  • The Orioles erupted for 8 runs in the 3rd inning en route to a 9-5 win over the Guardians. (GUARDIANS)
  • The Giants stormed back from a 1% win probability to beat the Cardinals 7-6, the day's biggest comeback. (CARDINALS)
  • Yoshinobu Yamamoto [7 IP, 1 ER, 10 K | 2.62 ERA, PAR 58] dominated as the Dodgers routed the Reds 14-1. (LAD)
  • The Pirates edged the White Sox 4-2 in the day's most dramatic game by WPA swing. (SOX)
  • Christopher Morel [3-4, 1 HR | RC 6] homered in the Mets' 15-14 slugfest win over the Marlins. (NYM)
  • The Tigers beat the Twins 7-2 and the Royals topped the Diamondbacks 5-2, with both clubs gaining ground in the AL Central race. (TWINS) (DIAMONDBACKS)
  • Will Warren [6 IP, 1 ER, 8 K | 4.05 ERA, PAR 30] shut down the Rockies as the Yankees won 6-1. (NYY)
