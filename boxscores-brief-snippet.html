Daily Brief

Pete Crow-Armstrong [4-6, 3 HR | RC 112]'s three-homer explosion powered the Cubs to a 17-5 rout of the Reds, but the bigger pennant race news came in the NL West, where both the Diamondbacks and Padres pulled within 9.0 games of the Dodgers after Detroit's upset of Los Angeles. In the AL wild card scramble, the Tigers, Royals, Orioles, and Blue Jays all gained ground with wins.

  • Pete Crow-Armstrong hit 3 home runs as the Cubs crushed the Reds 17-5, with Seiya Suzuki [3-5, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 82] and Alex Bregman [3-5, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 77] also going deep. (CHC)
  • The Red Sox and Yankees split their doubleheader, Boston taking game one behind Jake Bennett [6 IP, 0 ER, 7 K | 3.28 ERA, PAR 29]'s shutout before New York answered 9-2 in the nightcap. (BOS)
  • The Diamondbacks and Giants also split their doubleheader, with Merrill Kelly [7 IP, 0 ER, 7 K | 5.10 ERA, PAR 10] blanking San Francisco in game one before the Giants took game two 7-2. (AZ)
  • The Tigers stunned the Dodgers 2-1 despite Blake Snell [5 IP, 1 ER, 10 K | 2.42 ERA, PAR 11]'s 10-strikeout outing, while the Royals routed the Guardians 8-3. (LAD)
  • The Rays completed a stunning comeback from a 10% win probability to beat the Padres 7-6, the day's most dramatic finish. (PADRES)
  • Cristopher Sánchez [7 IP, 0 ER, 8 K | 2.52 ERA, PAR 67] threw seven shutout innings as the Phillies beat the Angels 4-2, and the Orioles topped the Athletics 5-3. (PHI)
  • The Pirates handled the Cardinals 6-2, the Mets beat the Astros 6-2 behind Nolan McLean [7 IP, 0 ER, 4 K | 3.07 ERA, PAR 46], and the Nationals edged the Marlins 5-4. (NYM)
  • The Braves nipped the Rockies 2-1, the White Sox beat the Twins 3 (SOX) (ROCKIES)
