Daily Brief

The AL Central race tightened dramatically as first-place Chicago White Sox lost ground to all four division rivals, with the Tigers, Royals, and Twins each gaining a full game. Meanwhile, the Reds and Cardinals split a doubleheader in Cincinnati, capped by a stunning Reds comeback from a 5% win probability.

  • The Reds rallied past the Cardinals 6-5 after dropping the opener 2-1, splitting a doubleheader that featured the day's biggest comeback. (CARDINALS)
  • Cristopher Sánchez [6 IP, 1 ER, 6 K | 2.51 ERA, PAR 61] led the Phillies past the Marlins 6-5, moving Philadelphia closer in the NL East chase. (PHI)
  • The Tigers beat the Pirates 8-5 behind Hao-Yu Lee [3-3, 2 BB | RC 27], while the Royals downed the Athletics 9-5 as Bobby Witt Jr. [3-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 69] homered — both teams gaining a full game on Chicago in the AL Central. (DET) (KC)
  • The Twins beat the Braves 4-2, further eroding Atlanta's NL East cushion as the Mets also topped the Padres 2-1 behind Nolan McLean [6 IP, 1 ER, 3 K | 3.35 ERA, PAR 40]. (NYM)
  • Pete Crow-Armstrong [4-5, 1 BB, 2 HR | RC 100] powered the Cubs past the crosstown White Sox 7-5, with Shota Imanaga [6 IP, 3 ER, 10 K | 3.77 ERA, PAR 29] striking out 10. (CHC)
  • Shohei Ohtani [4-5, 2 HR] homered twice as the Dodgers routed the Rockies 11-5, with Blake Snell [6 IP, 1 ER, 5 K | 3.60 ERA, PAR 6] turning in six strong innings. (LAD)
  • The Rays edged the Orioles 7-6 behind Yandy Díaz [2-3, 2 BB, 1 HR | RC 82], extending Baltimore's slide in the AL East. (TB)
  • The Red Sox crushed the Diamondbacks 11-1 at Fenway. (DIAMONDBACKS) (SOX)
