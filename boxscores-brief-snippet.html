Daily Brief

The AL East race tightened dramatically as the first-place Rays fell to the Braves while the Yankees routed the Rockies, cutting Tampa Bay's division lead to 3.0 games. Meanwhile, the Mariners pulled off the day's most improbable comeback, rallying from a 9% win probability to edge the Rangers 4-3.

  • Anthony Volpe [3-4, 1 HR | RC 20] and the Yankees crushed the Rockies 10-3, gaining a full game on the Rays in the AL East. (NYY)
  • The Mariners stunned the Rangers 4-3 after falling to a 9% win probability, dropping Texas 1 game in the AL West. (RANGERS)
  • Zack Wheeler [7 IP, 1 ER, 9 K | 3.14 ERA, PAR 42] was brilliant but the Phillies fell 2-1 to the Astros, with Cam Smith [2-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 54] homering to lose ground in the NL East. (PHI)
  • Martín Pérez [7 IP, 0 ER, 4 K | 3.09 ERA, PAR 34] tossed seven scoreless as the Braves beat the Rays 3-1, extending Atlanta's NL East lead. (ATL)
  • Jared Jones [7 IP, 0 ER, 8 K | 4.12 ERA, PAR 20] dominated with seven scoreless innings and Oneil Cruz [3-4, 1 HR | RC 57] homered as the Pirates blanked the White Sox 2-0, gaining on the Brewers in the NL Central. (PIT)
  • Jacob deGrom [6 IP, 0 ER, 12 K | 3.80 ERA, PAR 43] posted the day's top pitching line (6 IP, 0 ER, 12 K) in a losing effort for Texas. (TEX)
  • The AL Central leader White Sox lost ground to all four division rivals — Guardians, Tigers, Royals, and Twins. (SOX)
