Daily Brief

Both division leaders in the East stumbled Friday, as the Rays fell to the Orioles while the Yankees and Red Sox also won to gain ground, and the Braves dropped a tight one to the Brewers while the Phillies and Marlins closed the gap. The night's most dramatic finish came in Philadelphia, where the Phillies edged the Cardinals in a game that produced a 5.00 WPA swing.

  • Orioles beat the Rays, and both the Yankees (over Toronto) and Red Sox (over San Francisco) won to trim Tampa Bay's AL East lead. (YANKEES) (SOX) (RAYS)
  • Phillies outlasted the Cardinals 7-6 in the day's most dramatic game, while the Marlins edged the Nationals to also gain on Atlanta in the NL East. (CARDINALS) (NATIONALS)
  • Brewers knocked off the NL East-leading Braves 2-1 behind a strong outing from Jacob Misiorowski [6 IP, 0 ER, 6 K | 1.75 ERA, PAR 63]. (MIL)
  • Alex Bregman [3-5, 2 HR | RC 69] powered the Cubs with two homers, but Chicago still fell to the Mariners in a wild game. (CHC)
  • White Sox stormed back from an 8% win probability to stun the Mets 6-4, dealing New York another blow in the NL wild card race. (SOX)
  • MacKenzie Gore [6 IP, 0 ER, 7 K | 4.46 ERA, PAR 29] tossed six shutout innings as the Rangers nipped the Angels 2-1, pulling within a game of Houston in the AL West. (TEX)
  • Yoshinobu Yamamoto [6.1 IP, 2 ER, 9 K | 2.61 ERA, PAR 49] struck out nine as the Dodgers held off the Pirates 5-4. (LAD)
  • Guardians routed the Rockies 9-1 and the Diamondbacks blanked the Reds 9-0 in the day's most lopsided results. (GUARDIANS) (REDS)
