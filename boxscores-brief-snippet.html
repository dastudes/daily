Daily Brief

The Milwaukee Brewers clinched the NL pennant, finishing off the Los Angeles Dodgers in a four-game sweep behind a comeback from a 4-0 deficit. In the ALCS, Cleveland took a 2-1 series lead over Tampa Bay with a one-run win at Progressive Field.

  • Chandler Simpson [1-3 | RC 68]'s go-ahead triple in the top of the 4th scored two and swung the game Tampa's way, but Cleveland still edged the Rays 3-2 to take a 2-1 ALCS lead. (TB)
  • Will Smith [1-4 | RC 36]'s 8th-inning double, extended to a triple on a Sal Frelick [0-3, 1 BB | RC 32] error, pulled the Dodgers within one — but Milwaukee held on 6-4 to sweep the NLCS and reach the World Series. (LAD)
  • Gavin Williams [5 IP, 2 ER, 9 K | 3.76 ERA, PAR 49] was dominant for Cleveland, striking out nine over five innings. (CLE)
  • David Hamilton [2-4, 1 HR | RC 36] led the Brewers' offense with a two-hit night including a home run. (MIL)
  • José Ramírez [2-3, 1 BB | RC 56] went 2-for-3 and Petey Halpin [1-3, 1 HR | RC 16] homered in the Guardians' win. (CLE)
  • Garrett Mitchell [1-4, 1 HR | RC 68] and Shohei Ohtani [1-4, 1 HR] also went deep in the NLCS clincher. (MIL)
