Daily Brief

The Milwaukee Brewers took Game 1 of the World Series 3-2 over the Tampa Bay Rays at American Family Field, grabbing an early edge in the best-of-seven. The decisive moment came in the top of the ninth, with Tampa Bay threatening to tie before the rally was snuffed out.

  • Jonny DeLuca [0-4 | RC 45] struck out swinging to end the top of the 9th with the tying run stranded, sealing Milwaukee's 3-2 win in Game 1. (TB)
  • Jacob Misiorowski [5 IP, 1 ER, 3 K | 1.81 ERA, PAR 78] turned in the day's top pitching line for the Brewers over 5 innings. (MIL)
  • Nick Martinez [5 IP, 2 ER, 5 K | 3.10 ERA, PAR 49] matched him with a strong 5-inning start in the loss for Tampa Bay. (TB)
  • Jonathan Aranda [3-3, 1 BB | RC 90] led all hitters with a perfect 3-for-3 day for the Rays. (TB)
  • William Contreras [2-3, 1 BB | RC 80] chipped in a two-hit game for Milwaukee. (MIL)
