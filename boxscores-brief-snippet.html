Daily Brief

The Dodgers and Rays lost ground as division leaders on Monday, but the biggest shakeup came in the NL West, where Los Angeles saw Arizona, Colorado, and San Francisco all pick up a game. The Guardians-Angels game produced the day's largest WPA swing at 5.01, with Cleveland holding on for an 8-6 win.

  • Baltimore routed St. Louis 13-1, powered by an 8-run 5th inning, with Pete Alonso [4-4, 1 HR | RC 93] going 4-for-4 with a homer. (BAL)
  • Arizona completed a stunning comeback against the Cubs, rallying from a 3% win probability to beat Chicago 5-4. (CUBS)
  • Colorado, Arizona, and San Francisco all won to gain a game on the NL West-leading Dodgers, who fell 4-3 to Atlanta. (DODGERS)
  • Houston outslugged the Yankees 9-7 behind Yainer Diaz [3-4, 1 BB, 2 HR | RC 30]'s two-homer game, taking sole possession of first in the AL West as Texas lost. (HOU)
  • Pittsburgh gained a game in the NL Central with a 1-0 shutout of San Diego, backed by Paul Skenes [6 IP, 0 ER, 4 K | 3.79 ERA, PAR 42]' six scoreless innings. (PIT)
  • Michael King [7 IP, 0 ER, 7 K | 3.23 ERA, PAR 39] turned in the day's top pitching line with 7 scoreless in San Diego's loss, while George Kirby [7.2 IP, 1 ER, 9 K | 4.04 ERA, PAR 35] carried Seattle past Philadelphia 4-1. (SD) (SEA)
  • Mickey Gasper [2-2, 2 BB, 2 HR | RC 22] homered twice in Boston's 7-3 win over Miami, and the Red Sox gained in the AL East as the Rays fell to Detroit 4-1. (BOS)
  • The Mets edged Milwaukee 3-2, and the White Sox beat Texas 11-7 behind Tristan Peters [3-3, 2 BB, 1 HR | RC 56]' 3-for-3 day. (CWS)
