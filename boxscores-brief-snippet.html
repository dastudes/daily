Daily Brief

The AL Central race tightened dramatically as first-place Chicago fell while all three chasers won: the Tigers hammered Baltimore 14-0, the Twins edged Kansas City, and the Guardians split a doubleheader in Cincinnati. Meanwhile, the Yankees pulled within 1.5 games of the AL East-leading Rays, and both Arizona and San Diego gained ground on the Dodgers, who fell to Seattle in a game featuring a 4.66 WPA swing.

  • Eduardo Valencia [4-5, 2 HR | RC 12] powered Detroit's 14-0 rout of Baltimore, the Tigers gaining a full game in the AL Central chase. (DET)
  • Cleveland and Cincinnati split a doubleheader 6-5 and 2-0, with Gavin Williams [7 IP, 2 ER, 12 K | 3.72 ERA, PAR 35] dominant on the mound in the opener. (CLE)
  • Arizona edged Pittsburgh 8-7 and San Diego outlasted Colorado 8-7, both NL West chasers gaining a game on Los Angeles; Fernando Tatís Jr. went a perfect 5-for-5 for the Padres. (SD)
  • Seattle beat the Dodgers 7-6 despite a big night from Shohei Ohtani [3-4, 1 BB, 1 HR], with Dominic Canzone [2-4, 2 HR | RC 47] homering twice for the Mariners. (LAD)
  • Gerrit Cole [6 IP, 0 ER, 7 K | 3.57 ERA, PAR 18] tossed six shutout innings as the Yankees beat the White Sox 3-2 to close within 1.5 games in the AL East. (NYY)
  • Sandy Alcántara threw seven scoreless in Miami's 1-0 win over Philadelphia, tightening the NL East as Washington also beat Toronto 8-6. (MIA)
  • The Cubs routed St. Louis 10-2, the Brewers handled the Giants 8-2, Houston edged the Angels 3-2, Texas beat Tampa Bay (CUBS) (ANGELS) (BREWERS)
