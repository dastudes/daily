Daily Brief

The Braves erased an 80% win-probability deficit to beat the Phillies in the day's most dramatic game, pushing Philadelphia 6.0 back in the NL East with the regular season winding down. Elsewhere, the Yankees and Padres delivered shutout routs behind dominant starts.

  • Chris Sale [6.1 IP, 3 ER, 9 K | 2.16 ERA, PAR 68] and the Braves rallied past the Phillies 5-3 in the day's biggest comeback, with Michael Harris [1-2 | RC 18] II [3-3 | RC 93] leading the offense. (ATL)
  • Cam Schlittler [6.1 IP, 0 ER, 10 K | 1.96 ERA, PAR 80] dominated as the Yankees blanked the Red Sox 9-0, powered by a huge day from Ben Rice [4-5, 2 HR | RC 111]. (NYY)
  • Michael King [7 IP, 0 ER, 8 K | 3.21 ERA, PAR 48] tossed seven shutout innings as the Padres crushed the Cubs 8-0, with Fernando Tatis Jr. [2-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 105], Manny Machado [2-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 73], and Xander Bogaerts [2-4, 1 HR | RC 59] all going deep. (SD)
  • The White Sox beat the Astros 6-3 to pull within 1.0 game of the Guardians in the AL Central. (SOX) (ASTROS)
  • Jesús Luzardo [5 IP, 1 ER, 6 K | 2.86 ERA, PAR 63] turned in a strong start in the Phillies' loss. (PHI)
