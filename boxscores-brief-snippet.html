Daily Brief

The Tampa Bay Rays won the American League pennant, closing out the Cleveland Guardians in six games with a 7-3 victory at Tropicana Field. The decisive blow came early, as Tampa Bay built its lead in the second inning and never looked back.

  • Richie Palacios [2-4 | RC 43] opened the scoring with a two-run double in the bottom of the 2nd, staking the Rays to a 2-0 lead on the way to a 7-3 win that clinched the ALCS 4-2. (TB)
  • Freddy Peralta [5.1 IP, 1 ER, 3 K | 4.42 ERA, PAR 31] anchored the clincher with 5.1 innings of one-run ball. (TB)
  • Junior Caminero [2-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 116] homered and reached base three times to pace the Tampa Bay offense. (TB)
  • Yandy Díaz [1-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 98] added a two-run shot in the win. (TB)
  • Nathaniel Lowe [2-3, 1 BB | RC 54] had the lone standout night for Cleveland with a 2-for-3 effort. (CLE)
