Daily Brief

All three AL East and NL West division leaders lost ground Saturday, but the biggest shakeup came in the AL East, where both the Orioles and Yankees gained a full game on the Rays. Noah Cameron [9 IP, 0 ER, 8 K | 4.17 ERA, PAR 33] delivered the day's signature performance with a complete-game shutout in Kansas City's win at Anaheim.

  • Noah Cameron tossed a complete-game shutout as the Royals beat the Angels 3-0. (KC)
  • The Orioles routed the Rays 10-2 and the Yankees edged the Blue Jays 4-3, both cutting into Tampa Bay's AL East lead. (ORIOLES) (YANKEES)
  • The Phillies rallied from a 4% win probability to beat the Twins 7-5 in the day's biggest comeback. (PHILLIES)
  • The Rockies gained a game in the NL West with a 13-7 win over the Giants, while the Padres blanked the Guardians 5-0 to also close on the Dodgers, who fell 6-2 to the Brewers. (PADRES) (ROCKIES) (BREWERS)
  • The Mariners and Athletics both gained a game on the AL West-leading Astros, with Seattle winning 3-2 in Houston and the A's beating Texas 5-2. (ATHLETICS) (MARINERS)
  • Jordan Walker [3-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 79] led the Cardinals to an 11-4 win over the Cubs, costing Chicago a game in the NL Central. (STL)
  • Other results: Braves 5, Diamondbacks 3; Mets 4, Nationals 3; White Sox 7, Tigers 5; Marlins 7, Reds 1; Pirates 8, Red Sox 3. (SOX) (PIRATES) (DIAMONDBACKS) (NATIONALS) (MARLINS)
