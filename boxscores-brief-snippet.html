Daily Brief

The NL East contenders behind Atlanta made a move as the Braves lost to Washington, tightening a race Philadelphia now trails by 3.0 games. Meanwhile, the AL West got closer as Houston fell to the White Sox, allowing both Seattle and Texas to pick up a game on the division-leading Astros.

  • Kyle Isbel [0-3 | RC 24] and the Nationals beat Atlanta 9-5, cutting the Braves' NL East lead as Bryce Harper [4-4, 1 BB | RC 98] and the Phillies routed Arizona 7-1 and Miami downed Kansas City 6-3. (KC) (PHI)
  • Chicago's Sean Burke-led White Sox stunned Houston 5-1, while Texas topped the Athletics 8-5 and Seattle beat Boston 9-6 to close the AL West gap. (CWS)
  • Pittsburgh outslugged San Francisco 13-12 after a 9-run 3rd inning, with Brandon Lowe [4-6, 1 HR | RC 80] going 4-for-6 with a homer. (PIT)
  • Minnesota crushed Detroit 15-2 behind a 10-run 6th inning and a big day from Royce Lewis [3-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 47]. (MIN)
  • Gavin Williams [7 IP, 0 ER, 13 K | 3.81 ERA, PAR 42] dominated with 13 K over 7 shutout innings as Cleveland beat Toronto 6-1. (CLE)
  • Thomas Saggese [2-3, 1 BB, 2 HR | RC 9] homered twice as the Cardinals outslugged the Dodgers 13-8, gaining ground in the NL wild card race; Cincinnati also gained with a 4-3 win over San Diego. (STL)
  • Milwaukee stormed back from a 12% win probability to beat the Cubs 9-4 at Wrigley, the day's most dramatic comeback. (CUBS)
  • Colorado upset Baltimore 4-2, and the Yankees beat the Angels 7-3 behind a homer from Spencer Jones [2-3, 2 BB, 1 HR | RC 27]. (NYY)
