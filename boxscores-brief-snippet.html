Daily Brief

The National League contenders chipped away at both division leaders, with Washington, New York, Philadelphia, and Miami all winning to gain a game on Atlanta, while Cincinnati and Pittsburgh cut into Milwaukee's lead. The Athletics authored the day's most dramatic finish, rallying from a 2% win probability to beat the Angels 9-7.

  • The Athletics stunned the Angels 9-7 after falling to a 2% win probability. (ANGELS)
  • The Rays and Yankees split a doubleheader at Yankee Stadium, with New York taking the opener 2-0 behind Carlos Rodón [8 IP, 0 ER, 8 K | 2.68 ERA, PAR 27] before Tampa Bay answered 6-1. (NYY)
  • Cleveland edged Boston 3-2 at Fenway in the day's second-most dramatic game, dropping the Red Sox a full game in the AL wild card race. (SOX)
  • Miami routed the Cubs 8-2 in the day's biggest WPA swing, with Kyle Stowers [2-4, 2 BB, 1 HR | RC 67] homering. (MIA)
  • Jared Jones [7 IP, 0 ER, 9 K | 3.89 ERA, PAR 26] shut out the Cardinals over seven innings as Pittsburgh won 2-0. (PIT)
  • CJ Abrams [2-4, 2 HR | RC 95] hit two homers in Washington's 3-1 win over Detroit; Randal Grichuk [4-5, 1 HR | RC 46] and Chase Meidroth [3-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 79] each homered in the White Sox' 14-11 slugfest at Kansas City. (WSH) (CWS)
  • The Dodgers blanked the Padres 7-0, and Houston shut out Seattle 7-0 on the road. (PADRES)
  • Blue Jays at Orioles was postponed.
