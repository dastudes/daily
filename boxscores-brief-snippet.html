Daily Brief

The New York Mets pulled within a game of momentum in the NL East race with an 8-5 win at Truist Park, joining Philadelphia, Washington, and Miami in chipping away at Atlanta's lead. Meanwhile, the Rays continued to look dominant, erupting for eight runs in the eighth inning to bury the Athletics 10-6.

  • Tampa Bay scored 8 runs in the 8th inning en route to a 10-6 win over the Athletics, with Yandy Díaz [3-4, 1 HR | RC 76] leading the charge. (TB)
  • Francisco Lindor [3-5, 1 HR | RC 35] homered as the Mets beat the Braves 8-5, cutting into Atlanta's NL East lead. (NYM)
  • The Phillies edged the Cardinals 6-5 to gain a game in the NL East, while dealing STL a blow in the NL wild card race. (PHILLIES)
  • Dean Kremer [7 IP, 1 ER, 7 K | 5.25 ERA, PAR 4] was dominant in Minnesota's 9-5 win over Baltimore, backed by a 4-for-5 day from Austin Martin [4-5, 1 HR | RC 33]. (MIN)
  • MacKenzie Gore [5.1 IP, 1 ER, 9 K | 4.43 ERA, PAR 28] struck out 9 as Texas beat the Angels 4-1, despite a homer from Mike Trout [1-2, 3 BB, 1 HR | RC 62]; the Rangers sit just 0.5 GB in the AL West. (TEX)
  • Cam Smith [3-4, 1 HR | RC 46] paced Houston to a 6-3 win over the Giants at Oracle Park, keeping the Astros atop the AL West. (HOU)
  • The Dodgers held off Kansas City 6-5, the Blue Jays edged Boston 2-1, and San Diego topped Milwaukee 3-2. (JAYS) (DODGERS)
  • Arizona blanked Colorado 9-0 at Chase Field.
