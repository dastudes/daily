Daily Brief

Both AL East and NL West leaders lost ground as their pursuers won across the board. The Rays fell to the White Sox while Baltimore, Boston, Toronto, and New York all won, and the Dodgers dropped a 9-4 decision to Boston as Arizona, Colorado, and San Diego all gained a game in the NL West.

  • The Reds edged the Pirates 8-7 in the day's most dramatic game by WPA swing, gaining ground in the NL wild card race. (PIRATES)
  • Yainer Diaz [4-5, 1 HR | RC 24] and Isaac Paredes [2-3, 2 BB, 1 HR | RC 57] powered the Astros to an 11-2 rout of the Rangers. (HOU)
  • Will Warren [6.2 IP, 0 ER, 7 K | 4.16 ERA, PAR 23] tossed 6.2 shutout innings as the Yankees blanked the Cubs 2-0 at Wrigley. (NYY)
  • Dylan Cease [6 IP, 1 ER, 7 K | 2.42 ERA, PAR 48] dominated in Toronto's 3-1 win over St. Louis, extending his AL strikeout lead. (TOR)
  • The Orioles beat the Phillies 6-4 and the Red Sox topped the Dodgers 9-4, both gaining in the AL East chase. (PHILLIES) (SOX)
  • The Tigers hammered the Athletics 13-1, gaining a game in the AL wild card race. (TIGERS)
  • Tomoyuki Sugano [6.2 IP, 1 ER, 2 K | 4.49 ERA, PAR 12] turned in 6.2 strong innings as the Rockies beat the Royals 3-1. (COL)
  • The Padres shut out the Giants 7-0 and the Marlins beat the Mets 5-2, with Miami gaining in the NL wild card race. (MARLINS) (GIANTS)
