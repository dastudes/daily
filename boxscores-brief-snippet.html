Daily Brief

The AL Central race tightened as both the Tigers and Royals picked up a game on the division-leading White Sox, who were routed at Fenway. Meanwhile, the Rays and Rockies played the day's most dramatic contest, a 9-7 Tampa Bay win at Coors Field.

  • Troy Melton [7 IP, 0 ER, 4 K | 1.58 ERA, PAR 27] and the Tigers shut out the Mariners 8-0, gaining a game in the AL Central. (DET)
  • The Royals pounded the Twins 8-2, also gaining ground in the AL Central. (TWINS)
  • Jesús Luzardo [8 IP, 0 ER, 7 K | 3.37 ERA, PAR 43] dominated in the Phillies' 5-0 shutout of the Nationals, who lost ground in the NL East. (PHI)
  • Wilyer Abreu [2-4, 1 BB, 2 HR | RC 62] and Caleb Durbin [2-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 45] powered the Red Sox to a 14-2 rout of the White Sox. (BOS)
  • Ryan Weathers [6 IP, 0 ER, 6 K | 3.79 ERA, PAR 31] and the Yankees blanked the Cardinals 2-0 at Yankee Stadium. (NYY)
  • Luis Torrens [3-3, 1 BB, 1 HR] homered as the Mets beat the Guardians 6-2, gaining a game in the NL wild card race. (NYM)
  • The Padres routed the Diamondbacks 9-4, gaining a game on the Dodgers, who fell to Dansby Swanson [3-4, 1 HR | RC 45] and the Cubs 5-1. (CHC)
  • The Rays outlasted the Rockies 9-7 in the day's most dramatic game by WPA swing. (RAYS)
