Daily Brief

The Milwaukee Brewers moved to the brink of a World Series berth, taking a 3-0 stranglehold on the NLCS with a come-from-behind win at Dodger Stadium. Los Angeles now faces elimination in Game 4.

  • Jake Bauers [1-4, 1 HR | RC 90] flipped the game in the top of the 7th with a two-run homer that scored Christian Yelich [0-1 | RC 53], pushing Milwaukee ahead 3-2 en route to a 6-2 win. (MIL)
  • Robert Gasser [5 IP, 0 ER, 3 K | 3.88 ERA, PAR 24] was dominant in the start, delivering the top pitching line of the day. (MIL)
  • Blake Snell [6 IP, 4 ER, 9 K | 1.92 ERA, PAR 20] struck out nine over six innings but was tagged with four earned runs in the loss. (LAD)
  • Garrett Mitchell [1-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 68] added a solo homer in the seventh to extend the lead. (MIL)
  • Cooper Pratt [3-4 | RC 29] reached base three times with three hits in a strong supporting effort. (MIL)
