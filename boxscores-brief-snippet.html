Daily Brief

The Rays staved off elimination with a walk-off win in Game 4, but still trail the World Series 3-1 and face elimination again in Game 5. Tampa Bay fell as low as 17% win probability before rallying in the bottom of the 12th against Milwaukee.

  • Cooper Pratt [4-5, 1 HR | RC 29]'s solo homer in the top of the 12th put Milwaukee ahead 3-2, but the Rays answered in the bottom half to win 4-3 and avoid falling to a 3-1 series deficit from a different angle. (MIL)
  • Griffin Jax [4 IP, 2 ER, 6 K | 3.53 ERA, PAR 29] turned in a strong multi-inning outing for Tampa Bay, striking out six over four frames. (TB)
  • Aaron Ashby [2.1 IP, 0 ER, 3 K | 3.13 ERA, PAR 28] led all pitchers in PAR despite the Brewers' loss. (MIL)
  • Cooper Pratt also paced all hitters at the plate, going 4-for-5 with the go-ahead homer. (MIL)
  • Chandler Simpson [3-5 | RC 68] added three hits for the Rays in the win. (TB)
