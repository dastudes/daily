Daily Brief

The Cleveland Guardians clinched the AL Division Series with a 2-0 win over the Chicago White Sox, taking the best-of-five series 3-2 and eliminating Chicago. The decisive blow came from José Ramírez [1-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 56], whose fourth-inning homer provided all the runs Cleveland needed.

  • José Ramírez broke the scoreless tie with a two-run homer to right field in the bottom of the 4th, scoring Steven Kwan [1-3, 1 BB | RC 72] and giving Cleveland a 2-0 lead that held up as the series-clincher. (CLE)
  • Gavin Williams [5.2 IP, 0 ER, 12 K | 3.76 ERA, PAR 49] was dominant for Cleveland in the winner-take-all Game 5. (CLE)
  • Sean Burke [5 IP, 2 ER, 4 K | 3.35 ERA, PAR 48] pitched well in defeat for the White Sox. (CWS)
  • Cade Smith [1.2 IP, 0 ER, 4 K | 1.95 ERA, PAR 32] and Hunter Gaddis [1.1 IP, 0 ER, 4 K | 3.00 ERA, PAR 17] combined for three scoreless innings of relief to help close out the series. (CLE)
  • Petey Halpin [2-3 | RC 16] chipped in a multi-hit game for the Guardians. (CLE)
