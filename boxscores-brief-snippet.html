Daily Brief

The Rays evened the ALCS at two games apiece with a 7-3 win in Cleveland, flipping a game they'd fallen to 34% win probability in and setting up a pivotal Game 5 with the series knotted.

  • Junior Caminero [1-5 | RC 116]'s sixth-inning double to left scored Jonathan Aranda [3-4, 1 BB | RC 90] to put Tampa Bay ahead 2-1 for good in the Rays' 7-3 win at Progressive Field. (TB)
  • Nick Martinez [5.1 IP, 1 ER, 3 K | 3.10 ERA, PAR 49] turned in the night's top start, working into the sixth and holding Cleveland to one earned run. (TB)
  • Foster Griffin [3.2 IP, 1 ER, 5 K | 3.61 ERA, PAR 42] was sharp in a losing effort, striking out five over 3.2 innings. (CLE)
  • Travis Bazzana [2-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 61] led all hitters with a home run among his two hits for Cleveland. (CLE)
  • Jonathan Aranda and Richie Palacios [3-4 | RC 43] each went 3-for-4 at the top of the Rays' attack, with Yandy Díaz [2-5, 1 HR | RC 98] adding a homer. (TB)
