Daily Brief

The Milwaukee Brewers took Game 1 of the NLCS, rallying from an early deficit to beat the Dodgers 2-1 at American Family Field and grab a 1-0 series lead. The game turned on a late-innings defensive stand that preserved the one-run win.

  • Mookie Betts [1-4, 1 BB | RC 71] flied out to center fielder Garrett Mitchell [0-3, 1 BB | RC 68] in the top of the 9th, sealing Milwaukee's 2-1 Game 1 win over Los Angeles. (LAD)
  • Tarik Skubal [5 IP, 2 ER, 7 K | 2.73 ERA, PAR 59] was sharp over 5 innings for the Dodgers in a losing effort. (LAD)
  • Jacob Misiorowski [4.1 IP, 1 ER, 5 K | 1.81 ERA, PAR 78] matched him with a strong start for Milwaukee. (MIL)
  • Brice Turang [1-2, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 96] supplied the key offense for the Brewers with a home run. (MIL)
  • The Brewers' bullpen trio of Aaron Ashby [2 IP, 0 ER, 1 K | 3.13 ERA, PAR 28], Abner Uribe [1 IP, 0 ER, 2 K | 1.98 ERA, PAR 24], and company shut the door late. (MIL)
