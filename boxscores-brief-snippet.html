Daily Brief

The National League Central race tightened as both the Cubs and Reds won while the division-leading Brewers lost ground, and the Phillies pulled within striking distance in the NL East by beating the Braves. In the day's most dramatic finish, the Diamondbacks rallied from a 14% win probability to stun the Astros and cut into Houston's shrinking AL West lead.

  • Ketel Marte [1-5, 1 HR | RC 69] and the Diamondbacks completed a stunning comeback to beat the Astros 4-3, with Arizona gaining ground in the NL West chase. (AZ)
  • Zack Wheeler [6 IP, 1 ER, 9 K | 3.24 ERA, PAR 39] and the Phillies beat the Braves 4-2, closing to within 4 games in the NL East. (PHI)
  • Riley Greene [4-5, 2 HR | RC 74] homered twice as Framber Valdez [7 IP, 0 ER, 3 K | 4.18 ERA, PAR 32] tossed seven shutout innings in the Tigers' 6-0 win over the Guardians, gaining a game in the AL Central. (DET)
  • Sonny Gray [7 IP, 0 ER, 7 K | 2.70 ERA, PAR 48] blanked the Orioles over seven innings as the Red Sox won 5-0 at Camden Yards. (BOS)
  • Jeffrey Springs [7 IP, 0 ER, 6 K | 6.02 ERA, PAR -1] dominated in the Athletics' 6-2 win over the Mariners, and Drew Rasmussen [7 IP, 1 ER, 7 K | 2.88 ERA, PAR 53] was nearly as sharp in the Rays' 6-3 victory in Texas. (ATH) (TB)
  • Rafael Devers [2-4, 2 HR | RC 95] and Christian Koss [2-4, 2 HR] each homered twice as the Giants beat the Mets 9-5, costing New York half a game in the NL wild card race. (SF)
  • The Cubs edged the Marlins 6-5 and the Reds beat the Brewers 5-3, with both clubs gaining a game on Milwaukee in the NL Central. (CUBS) (BREWERS)
  • The Blue Jays outlasted the Royals 4-3 in one of the day's most dramatic finishes, while the Dodgers held off the Nationals 6-5. (JAYS) (NATIONALS)
