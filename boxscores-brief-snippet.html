Daily Brief

All three AL East contenders behind Tampa Bay gained ground as the Rays lost to Baltimore, with Toronto, Boston, and the Orioles all winning to tighten the division. Meanwhile, the NL East saw Atlanta lose to Milwaukee while all three chasers — Philadelphia, Miami, and the Mets — picked up wins.

  • Andrew Painter [7 IP, 1 ER, 8 K | 6.06 ERA, PAR 5] led Philadelphia to a rout of St. Louis, gaining a game on Atlanta in the NL East. (PHI)
  • JJ Bleday [4-6, 1 HR | RC 54] hit for the cycle as Cincinnati beat Arizona, with Elly De La Cruz [4-6, 1 HR | RC 72] and Héctor Rodríguez [2-3, 2 BB, 1 HR | RC 3] also homering. (CIN)
  • Seattle staged a stunning comeback against the Cubs, falling to a 2% win probability before rallying behind Randy Arozarena [3-4, 1 BB, 2 HR | RC 77]'s two-homer day. (SEA)
  • Baltimore edged Tampa Bay in the day's fifth-biggest WPA swing, part of a three-team gain on the AL East leaders.
  • Ryan Johnson [7 IP, 0 ER, 3 K | 5.77 ERA, PAR 0] shut out Texas as the Angels gained a game on Houston in the AL West; the Athletics also beat the Astros behind Jacob Lopez [6 IP, 1 ER, 9 K | 4.98 ERA, PAR 11]. (LAA) (ATH)
  • Michael Wacha [7 IP, 1 ER, 6 K | 3.50 ERA, PAR 39] helped Kansas City beat Detroit, while Cleveland won at Coors Field — both clubs gaining on the White Sox in the AL Central. (KC)
  • Boston edged San Francisco in the day's largest WPA swing (3.87), with Andrew Knizner [3-4, 1 HR | RC 2] homering for the Giants in the loss. (SF)
  • Tarik Skubal [7 IP, 3 ER, 11 K | 2.92 ERA, PAR 43] struck out 11 as the Dodgers held off Pittsburgh. (LAD)
