Daily Brief

The Pirates' 7-4 win over Milwaukee cut into the Brewers' NL Central lead, with Chicago and St. Louis also gaining a half-game on the division leader. Meanwhile, Boston pulled off the day's most improbable rally, climbing back from a 9% win probability to beat Texas 4-3 and deal the Rangers a costly loss in the AL wild card race.
