Daily Brief

The White Sox knocked off the Yankees 2-1 in the day's most dramatic game by WPA swing, dropping New York another game back in the AL East while Tampa Bay's win over Texas kept the Rays comfortably atop the division. Meanwhile, the NL Central race tightened as both the Cubs and Reds gained half a game on the idle Brewers.

  • Sean Burke [6 IP, 0 ER, 10 K | 3.05 ERA, PAR 39] and the White Sox stifled the Yankees in a 2-1 win, the day's biggest WPA swing. (CWS)
  • The Cubs beat the Cardinals 4-2 and the Reds edged the Pirates 3-2, each gaining 0.5 games on Milwaukee in the NL Central. (CUBS) (PIRATES)
  • Tampa Bay held off Texas 3-2, while the Angels and Astros both gained half a game on the Rangers in the AL West. (RANGERS)
  • Minnesota beat Kansas City 4-3, dropping the Royals a full game in the AL Central and boosting the Twins in the AL wild card race. (ROYALS)
  • Atlanta topped Washington 5-4 and the Mets beat Miami 4-2, sending both the Nationals and Marlins a game back in the NL East. (MARLINS) (NATIONALS)
  • Daylen Lile [2-4, 2 HR | RC 52] homered twice for Washington in a losing effort, posting the day's top LWTS mark. (WSH)
  • Noah Cameron [8 IP, 0 ER, 7 K | 4.61 ERA, PAR 22] threw 8 shutout innings for Kansas City but got no decision as the Royals fell. (KC)
  • The Dodgers beat Seattle 6-2 and the Giants shut down San Diego 4-1, with Teoscar Hernández [2-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 34] and Robbie Ray [6 IP, 1 ER, 6 K | 3.09 ERA, PAR 31] among the day's standouts. (LAD) (SF)
