Daily Brief

The Phillies pulled off the day's most dramatic result, rallying from an 11% win probability to beat the first-place Braves 4-3 at Truist Park in the game with the biggest WPA swing of the day. The loss doesn't shake Atlanta's grip on the NL East, but Philadelphia closed to within 6.0 games with the comeback win.

  • Cristopher Sánchez [6.2 IP, 3 ER, 6 K | 2.91 ERA, PAR 72] added a strong 6.2-inning start in Philadelphia's comeback win, and Andrew Painter [3 IP, 0 ER, 3 K | 5.42 ERA, PAR 13] turned in 3 scoreless innings of relief to earn the win. (PHI)
