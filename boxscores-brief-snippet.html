Daily Brief

The Braves pulled off the day's most consequential result, rallying from a 12% win probability to beat the Phillies 6-5 and push their NL East lead to 5.0 games. In the AL Central, all three chasers gained on the White Sox, tightening the division to a half-game race between Chicago and Cleveland.

  • Ronald Acuña Jr. [1-5 | RC 54] and the Braves stunned the Phillies 6-5 in the day's biggest comeback, trimming Philadelphia's deficit hopes in the NL East. (ATL)
  • The Brewers demolished the Reds 20-0, powered by an 8-run 5th inning, with Dustin May [6 IP, 0 ER, 6 K | 4.50 ERA, PAR 31] tossing 6 shutout innings and Brice Turang [3-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 83] leading all hitters. (MIL)
  • The Guardians, Tigers and Royals all won, each gaining a game on the AL Central-leading White Sox, who lost 7-3 to the Cardinals. (TIGERS) (ROYALS) (SOX) (GUARDIANS)
  • The Athletics edged the Mariners 6-5, gaining ground in the AL West while Seattle slipped in the wild card chase. (MARINERS)
  • Carlos Rodón [6.1 IP, 1 ER, 8 K | 2.95 ERA, PAR 21] and the Yankees beat the Mets 6-4 in the Subway Series, with Ben Rice [3-4, 1 HR | RC 98] homering. (NYY)
  • The Rays kept pace atop the AL East as Drew Rasmussen [7 IP, 1 ER, 4 K | 2.81 ERA, PAR 56] dominated the Astros over 7 innings in a 3-1 win. (TB)
  • The Dodgers rolled past the Marlins 6-2 behind a big day from Kyle Tucker [3-3, 1 HR | RC 60]. (LAD)
  • The Diamondbacks routed the Rangers 9-1, and the Padres beat the Giants 7-5 to stay in the NL wild card mix. (RANGERS) (PADRES)
