Daily Brief

The AL West remains deadlocked entering the final weekend, as both the Astros and Rangers won to stay tied at 79-80. Houston's victory was powered by Lucas Spence [3-4, 3 HR | RC 5]'s three-homer explosion, while Texas quietly kept pace by handling the Mets.

  • Lucas Spence hit 3 home runs to lead the Astros past the Athletics, keeping Houston tied atop the AL West. (HOU)
  • The Rangers beat the Mets behind Josh Jung [3-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 67]'s perfect day to remain tied with Houston. (TEX)
  • The Guardians edged the Red Sox 1-0 to hold a 1.0-game lead in the AL Central; the White Sox stayed within striking distance by routing the Royals, with David Sandlin [6 IP, 1 ER, 7 K | 5.61 ERA, PAR 4] and Kyle Teel [2-2, 3 BB, 1 HR | RC 13] starring. (CWS)
  • The Phillies fell to the Brewers but continue to hold the final NL wild card spot. (BREWERS)
  • The Diamondbacks outslugged the Rockies 12-8 but remain 2.0 games back in the NL wild card race with only 3 to play. (DIAMONDBACKS)
  • The Yankees roared back from a 9% win probability to beat the Rays 6-4 in the day's most dramatic comeback. (RAYS)
  • The Reds edged the Braves 7-6 in the largest WPA swing of the day; the Cubs, Pirates, and Padres also picked up narrow wins. (PIRATES) (CUBS) (REDS) (PADRES)
