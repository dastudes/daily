Daily Brief

The Chicago Cubs gained a game on the Milwaukee Brewers in the NL Central after blasting Seattle 19-2, highlighted by a nine-run eighth inning. Milwaukee dropped its matchup with Atlanta, trimming its division lead to six games, while the Athletics also picked up ground on the AL West co-leaders after edging Houston.

  • Cubs pounded the Mariners 19-2, erupting for nine runs in the eighth inning; Ian Happ [2-4, 2 BB, 1 HR | RC 63] homered in the rout. (CHC)
  • Braves beat the Brewers 4-2 in the day's most dramatic game by WPA swing, cutting into Milwaukee's NL Central lead. (BRAVES)
  • Phillies stormed back from a 1% win probability to defeat the Cardinals 6-4, the day's biggest comeback. (CARDINALS)
  • Athletics edged the Astros 7-6, gaining a game in the AL West race. (ATHLETICS)
  • Padres routed the Twins 8-1 behind Fernando Tatis Jr. [4-5, 2 HR | RC 82]'s two-homer day and six strong innings from Walker Buehler [6 IP, 1 ER, 4 K | 4.64 ERA, PAR 22]. (SD)
  • Rangers topped the Angels 5-3 as Cal Quantrill [6 IP, 0 ER, 6 K | 2.92 ERA, PAR 23] tossed six shutout innings. (TEX)
  • Royals outslugged the Tigers 11-7, with Nick Loftin [3-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 36] and John Rave [2-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 8] both homering. (KC)
  • Other winners: Rays over Orioles, Yankees over Blue Jays, Marlins over Nationals, White Sox over Mets, Guardians over Rockies, Red Sox over Giants, Dodgers over Pirates, Diamondbacks over Reds. (RAYS) (JAYS) (NATIONALS) (METS) (SOX) (GUARDIANS) (PIRATES) (REDS)
