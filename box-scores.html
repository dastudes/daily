Daily Brief

The AL East and AL West races tightened dramatically, as Baltimore swept a doubleheader from Toronto to gain 1.5 games on the Rays, while Seattle rallied from a 16% win probability to beat Houston 6-5, pulling within 4 games in the AL West as both co-leaders Houston and Texas lost. In the NL West, both the Padres and Diamondbacks gained a game on the Dodgers, who fell 5-1 to San Diego.

  • Seattle Mariners staged the day's biggest comeback, beating Houston 6-5 after falling to 16% win probability, gaining a game in the AL West. (SEATTLE MARINERS)
  • Baltimore Orioles swept a doubleheader from Toronto, winning both games 4-2 and gaining 1.5 games in the AL wild card race. (BALTIMORE ORIOLES)
  • San Diego Padres beat the Los Angeles Dodgers 5-1, closing the NL West gap. (SAN DIEGO PADRES)
  • New York Yankees routed Tampa Bay 9-2 behind Ben Rice [3-4, 1 BB, 2 HR | RC 109], who homered twice and reached 41 on the season to tie for the AL lead. (NYY)
  • Boston edged Cleveland 1-0 as Sonny Gray [6 IP, 0 ER, 3 K | 2.72 ERA, PAR 55] tossed six shutout innings, cutting into the Guardians' AL Central lead. (BOS)
  • Kansas City beat Chicago 5-4 with Maikel Garcia [3-3, 1 BB | RC 37] going 3-for-3; Cleveland's Central lead over the White Sox held at 1 game. (KC)
  • Milwaukee beat Philadelphia 4-1, and Atlanta edged Cincinnati 3-2 as Chris Sale [5.2 IP, 1 ER, 10 K | 2.16 ERA, PAR 68] struck out 10 over 5.2 innings. (ATL)
  • Washington beat Detroit 4-2, St. Louis topped Pittsburgh 5-1, the Mets beat Texas 7-2 behind a Juan Soto [1-2, 3 BB, 1 HR | RC 80] homer (NYM)
