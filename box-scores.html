Daily Brief

The AL Central race tightened dramatically as the Tigers and Twins both gained a full game on the division-leading White Sox, who barely survived an 8.80 WPA swing at Fenway. Detroit's shutout in Seattle and Minnesota's one-run win in Kansas City leave the White Sox clinging to a 3.0-game lead in the majors' most crowded division.

  • Chase Meidroth [3-5, 2 BB, 2 HR | RC 58] homered twice for the White Sox, but Boston edged Chicago 12-11 in the day's most dramatic game, gaining a half-game on Tampa Bay in the AL East. (CWS)
  • Framber Valdez [7 IP, 0 ER, 5 K | 4.18 ERA, PAR 27] tossed seven shutout innings as the Tigers blanked the Mariners 11-0, pulling within 3.5 of the AL Central lead. (DET)
  • The Twins beat the Royals 4-3 to also gain a game in the AL Central; Kansas City lost ground in the AL wild card race. (TWINS)
  • Dylan Cease [7 IP, 0 ER, 10 K | 2.28 ERA, PAR 53] struck out 10 over seven scoreless innings but the Blue Jays fell 3-2 to the Cubs, who gained a game in the NL wild card race. (TOR)
  • The Brewers beat the Pirates 5-2 behind Dustin May [6 IP, 2 ER, 5 K | 4.30 ERA, PAR 28], maintaining Milwaukee's grip on the NL Central. (MIL)
  • The Padres defeated the Diamondbacks 5-1, closing to 9.5 games behind the Dodgers in the NL West and gaining ground on Arizona. (PADRES)
  • The Braves routed the Marlins 11-3 with Matt Olson [2-3, 2 BB, 1 HR | RC 85], Mauricio Dubón [4-4, 1 BB | RC 53], and Ronald Acuña Jr. [2-5, 2 HR | RC 37] all posting big days. (ATL)
  • The Mets pounded the Guardians 13-6 behind Marcus Semien [3-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 34], and the Reds edged the Athletics 6-5; both winners gained a game in the NL wild card (NYM)
