Daily Brief

All four division leaders lost ground on Thursday, with the Dodgers, Braves, White Sox, and Brewers each dropping games while their closest pursuers won. The Blue Jays' one-run win over the Phillies produced the day's biggest WPA swing at 4.15.

  • Cam Smith [3-4, 2 HR | RC 43] powered Houston past San Diego, further denting the Padres' wild card position. (HOU)

  • Cleveland and Minnesota both won to pull within 2.0 games of the White Sox in the AL Central, as the Cubs and Cardinals each gained on Milwaukee in the NL Central. (SOX) (CUBS) (CARDINALS)
  • Payton Tolle [6 IP, 1 ER, 14 K | 3.20 ERA, PAR 33] dominated in Boston's 13-1 rout of the Athletics, matching Tampa Bay's Drew Rasmussen [7 IP, 0 ER, 8 K | 2.76 ERA, PAR 43] atop the day's pitching leaderboard as the Rays edged Seattle. (BOS) (TB)
  • Max Fried [6.1 IP, 0 ER, 7 K | 2.89 ERA, PAR 30] and the Yankees shut down Atlanta in a 3-2 win at Yankee Stadium. (NYY)
  • Texas beat Baltimore 2-1 behind a strong outing from Shane Baz [8 IP, 2 ER, 9 K | 3.77 ERA, PAR 37], and the Angels edged the Marlins 4-3 to knock Miami back in the NL wild card race. (BAL)
