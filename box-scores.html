Daily Brief

The Guardians evened the AL Division Series 2-2 with a 9-5 win at Rate Field, erasing a 4-3 deficit entering the sixth to force a decisive Game 5 with both teams facing elimination. The comeback was keyed by José Ramírez [2-5, 1 HR | RC 56]'s go-ahead homer, the game's decisive swing.

  • José Ramírez flipped the game with a top-5th two-run homer that scored Steven Kwan [1-3, 2 BB | RC 72], putting Cleveland ahead 3-2 en route to the 9-5 win and a 2-2 series tie. (CLE)
  • Patrick Bailey [2-3, 1 HR | RC 28] added a homer and reached base twice as part of Cleveland's rally. (CLE)
  • Nathaniel Lowe [2-4, 1 BB | RC 54] chipped in a multi-hit day in the win. (CLE)
  • Shawn Armstrong [1.2 IP, 0 ER, 2 K | 4.62 ERA, PAR 6] and Hunter Gaddis [1.1 IP, 0 ER, 1 K | 3.00 ERA, PAR 17] combined for three scoreless innings of relief to protect the lead. (CLE)
