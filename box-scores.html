Daily Brief

The Milwaukee Brewers took a commanding 2-0 lead in the NLCS with a win over the Dodgers, while the Guardians stole Game 1 of the ALCS from the Rays in Tampa after trailing late. Cleveland's comeback produced the day's biggest WPA swing.

  • Christian Yelich [2-5 | RC 53]'s two-run single in the bottom of the 3rd put Milwaukee ahead for good, and the Brewers beat the Dodgers 7-4 to go up 2-0 in the NLCS. (MIL)
  • Chase DeLauter [3-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 82]'s go-ahead double in the top of the 8th capped Cleveland's rally from a 2-1 deficit, lifting the Guardians over the Rays 7-3 for a 1-0 ALCS lead. (CLE)
  • Drew Rasmussen [6 IP, 2 ER, 7 K | 2.67 ERA, PAR 62] was dominant in the loss, posting the day's top pitching line for Tampa Bay. (TB)
  • William Contreras [3-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 80] led the Brewers' offense with a 3-for-4 night and a home run. (MIL)
  • Patrick Bailey [2-4, 1 HR | RC 28] homered as part of Cleveland's attack, backing DeLauter's big game. (CLE)
