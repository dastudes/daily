Daily Brief

Both NL Division Series shifted on Tuesday, with the Dodgers taking a 2-1 lead over the Braves and the Padres cutting the Brewers' lead to 2-1. Atlanta and San Diego now face elimination in Game 4.

  • Enrique Hernández [1-4, 1 HR]'s two-run homer in the top of the 4th broke the game open as the Dodgers beat the Braves 3-1 to take a 2-1 series lead. (LAD)
  • Christian Yelich [2-4, 1 BB | RC 53]'s 9th-inning double play ended the Brewers' rally, giving the Padres a 4-3 win and cutting Milwaukee's series lead to 2-1. (MIL)
  • Yoshinobu Yamamoto [7 IP, 1 ER, 10 K | 2.53 ERA, PAR 63] dominated Atlanta over 7 innings, leading all pitchers in PAR on the day. (LAD)
  • William Contreras [3-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 80] had the top batting line of the day in a losing effort for Milwaukee. (MIL)
  • Jake Cronenworth [2-2, 1 HR | RC 37] homered in the Padres' comeback win. (SD)
  • Chris Sale [5 IP, 3 ER, 7 K | 2.16 ERA, PAR 68] turned in a strong start for the Braves but took the loss. (ATL)
