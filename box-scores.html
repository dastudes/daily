Daily Brief

Both NL division leaders lost ground Sunday, as the Brewers and Dodgers dropped games while their pursuers won. Milwaukee fell to the Angels while the Reds and Cardinals both won, and Los Angeles was beaten at home by Boston as the Padres and Rockies also gained ground in the NL West.

  • Kyle Freeland [9 IP, 1 ER, 8 K | 6.83 ERA, PAR 3] threw a complete game as the Rockies beat the Royals 8-1, cutting into Colorado's deficit in the NL West. (COL)
  • Wilyer Abreu [2-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 59] and the Red Sox knocked off the Dodgers 8-4, pulling Boston within striking distance in the AL East. (BOS)
  • The Reds routed the Pirates 10-2 behind Tyler Stephenson [2-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 38], gaining a game on Milwaukee in the NL Central. (CIN)
  • Matthew Liberatore [6 IP, 0 ER, 7 K | 4.99 ERA, PAR 14] led the Cardinals past the Blue Jays 5-1, another blow to Milwaukee's division lead and Toronto's wild card hopes. (STL)
  • The Yankees edged the Cubs 2-1 at Wrigley in one of the day's most dramatic games, gaining on the Rays in the AL East. (RAYS) (YANKEES)
  • George Kirby [7 IP, 0 ER, 3 K | 3.76 ERA, PAR 33] and the Mariners outlasted the Twins 7-6 in the day's biggest WPA swing. (SEA)
  • Jackson Merrill [2-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 48] homered as the Padres beat the Giants 5-4, gaining ground on the Dodgers in the NL West. (SD)
  • Brandon Nimmo [2-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 60] homered for Texas, but the Rangers fell to the Astros 7-3 behind Jeremy Peña [2-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 51]. (TEX)
