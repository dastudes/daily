Daily Brief

The AL East race tightened dramatically as the first-place Rays fell to the Braves while the Yankees routed the Rockies, cutting Tampa Bay's division lead to 3.0 games.   • Jacob deGrom [6 IP, 0 ER, 12 K | 3.80 ERA, PAR 43] posted the day's top pitching line (6 IP, 0 ER, 12 K) in a losing effort for Texas. (TEX)
  • The AL Central leader White Sox lost ground to all four division rivals — Guardians, Tigers, Royals, and Twins. (SOX)
