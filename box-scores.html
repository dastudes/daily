Daily Brief

The Milwaukee Brewers took a commanding 2-0 lead over the San Diego Padres in their NL Division Series, rallying from a deep deficit to pull off the day's biggest comeback and push the Padres to the brink of elimination. In the other NLDS, the Atlanta Braves evened their series with the Los Angeles Dodgers at 1-1 with a 3-2 road win.

  • Milwaukee defeated San Diego 4-3 at American Family Field, erasing a deep deficit to seize a 2-0 series lead; the Padres face elimination in Game 3. (PADRES)
  • Atlanta beat Los Angeles 3-2 at Dodger Stadium to tie their NLDS at 1-1.
  • Michael King [5 IP, 1 ER, 6 K | 3.21 ERA, PAR 48] turned in the day's best pitching line for San Diego in a losing effort, while Logan Henderson [5 IP, 1 ER, 4 K | 2.48 ERA, PAR 34] was strong for the Brewers. (SD)
  • Didier Fuentes [3.2 IP, 1 ER, 3 K | 2.46 ERA, PAR 28] and reliever Ray Kerr [2.2 IP, 0 ER, 4 K | 1.49 ERA, PAR 7] anchored Atlanta's pitching effort in the win. (ATL)
  • Freddie Freeman [2-4, 1 HR | RC 90] homered and led all batters at the plate, and Max Muncy [1-3, 1 HR | RC 83] also went deep for the Dodgers in the loss. (LAD)
  • Michael Harris II [3-5 | RC 93] paced the Braves' offense with a three-hit game. (ATL)
