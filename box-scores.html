Daily Brief

Both NL division leaders lost ground Tuesday as the Braves and Brewers watched their pursuers gain. Atlanta fell in Minnesota while Washington, New York, and Philadelphia all won to close the NL East gap, and in the Central, Cincinnati and Pittsburgh both gained on Milwaukee.

  • Cade Cavalli [6 IP, 0 ER, 11 K | 3.23 ERA, PAR 43] dominated as the Nationals shut out the Rangers 6-0, with Andrés Chaparro [3-4, 1 HR | RC 23] and José Tena [2-3, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 28] homering in the win. (WSH)
  • The Diamondbacks edged the Red Sox 7-6 in the day's most dramatic game, a 4.80 WPA swing that cost Boston ground in the AL East. (SOX)
  • The Royals staged the day's biggest comeback, rallying from a 7% win probability to beat the Athletics 9-7 behind Vinnie Pasquantino [3-4, 1 BB, 1 HR | RC 37]. (KC)
  • Rafael Flores Jr. [2-2, 2 HR] homered twice as the Pirates beat the Tigers 4-3, gaining a game in the NL Central race. (PIT)
  • Aaron Nola [7 IP, 1 ER, 8 K | 5.12 ERA, PAR 15] tossed seven strong innings as the Phillies beat the Marlins 4-1 to gain in the NL East. (PHI)
  • José Urquidy [6 IP, 0 ER, 8 K | 5.06 ERA, PAR 4] shut out the Cubs across six innings in the White Sox's 3-0 crosstown win at Wrigley. (CWS)
  • The Mets beat the Padres 4-2, and the Twins knocked off the Braves 6-4, both trimming NL East and wild card gaps. (PADRES) (BRAVES)
  • The Yankees beat the Orioles 5-3, the Giants blanked the Guardians 1-0 behind Adrian Houser [5 IP, 0 ER, 3 K | 4.08 ERA, PAR 20], and the Dodgers held off the Rockies 6-4. (SF)
